## 01 / About Me

I'm **Adriel**, a third-year Mechatronics Engineering undergraduate at the **Federal University of Technology, Minna, Nigeria**.

I'm interested in how intelligent machines move, sense, interact with their environment, and make decisions.

My work spans custom PCB design, embedded electronics, mechanical CAD, and software development. I'm currently developing my skills in **ROS 2, robotic manipulation, SolidWorks, and computer architecture**.

I enjoy understanding systems from the ground up: from electronic components and mechanical mechanisms to the software that connects them.

> **Engineering philosophy:** Understand the fundamentals. Build something tangible. Test it. Document what works — and what doesn't.

## 03 / Selected Engineering Projects

### DevvyBoard — Custom RP2040 Development Board

**Embedded Systems · KiCad · PCB Design · RP2040**

A custom microcontroller development board created to understand how the supporting electronics of an RP2040-based system fit together.

- Designed a two-layer PCB with USB-C, SPI flash, clock circuitry and power regulation.
- Prepared design and manufacturing files.
- Documented PCB assembly and basic electrical checks.
- Explored the complete process from schematic to assembled hardware.

**[Explore DevvyBoard →](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/devvyboard)**

### Aries — CO₂ Air Quality Monitor

**ESP32-C3 · SCD40 · KiCad · Sensors · Mechanical Enclosure**

A compact air-quality monitoring device project combining electronics, sensing and product design.

- Designed around a Seeed XIAO ESP32-C3 and Sensirion SCD40 sensor.
- Incorporated a circular TFT display and battery-powered architecture.
- Developed PCB layouts and enclosure designs.
- Documented the component selection and hardware architecture.

**[Explore Aries →](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/aries)**

### Hot Hermes — Temperature Monitoring System

**XIAO RP2040 · AHT20 · OLED · I2C**

A temperature and humidity monitoring project with a display and configurable buzzer alert.

- Integrated an AHT20 sensor with a microcontroller.
- Worked with I2C communication and an OLED display.
- Designed the supporting electronics and enclosure.

**[Explore Hot Hermes →](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/hot-hermes)**

### Oa — USB Hub PCB

**EasyEDA · USB · PCB Design · Hardware Architecture**

A four-port USB hub project designed around the SL2.1S controller.

- Created schematic and PCB layouts.
- Revised the clock circuitry and USB routing.
- Prepared manufacturing information and documented design changes.

**[Explore Oa →](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/oa_usb_hub)**

### Robotic Arm and Gripper — CAD Study

**SolidWorks · Mechanical Assemblies · Mechanisms**

A tutorial-guided mechanical CAD study of an articulated robotic arm and gripper.

- Practiced assembly modeling and component positioning.
- Worked with mates and mechanical relationships.
- Explored articulated links and gripper geometry.
- Continuing to study mechanism design and motion.

*CAD study documentation and project files to be published.*
