# Adriel Babalola

Mechatronics Engineering undergraduate at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, Nigeria (CGPA 4.89/5.00).

I build robotic, embedded, and sensing systems, from PCB design and sensor integration to mechanical CAD and software. My current focus is robot manipulation and embodied intelligence.

## Focus

- **Robotics:** ROS 2 fundamentals, URDF and simulation, teleoperation with OpenCV and MediaPipe
- **Electronics and embedded:** KiCad PCB design, RP2040, ESP32-C3, I2C, SPI, sensor integration
- **Mechanical design:** SolidWorks assemblies and mechanisms, 3D-printed enclosures
- **Software:** Python, C++, TypeScript

## Projects

- **[DevvyBoard](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/devvyboard)**: Custom two-layer RP2040 development board designed in KiCad, with USB-C, SPI flash, a 3.3 V regulator, and boot/reset circuitry.
- **[Aries](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/aries)**: Battery-powered CO2 monitor with an SCD40 sensor, ESP32-C3, circular TFT display, and custom PCB.
- **[Hot Hermes](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/hot-hermes)**: Temperature and humidity monitor with an AHT20 sensor and OLED display.
- **[Ore](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/Ore)**: Voice-first Yoruba assistant for users with limited typing ability.
- **[Neura](https://github.com/adriel-babalola/neura)**: Adaptive STEM tutor for low-bandwidth learning.
